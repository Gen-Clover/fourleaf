# Gen Clover — USA Client Rate Card & Pricing Calculator (2026)

**Scope:** India-based senior talent (7+ years' experience) delivering to US clients
**Positioning:** Gen Clover is a managed product and engineering vendor, not a body-shop staffing firm.
**Version:** Final v2 (September 2026). Standard rates re-based on market position, role ladder and delivery-cost check.

## 1. Pricing Formula (Internal)

**Core model:** 65% Delivery + 10% Growth/Talent + 25% Gen Clover Corporate

| Bucket | % of Client Revenue | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery allocation | **65%** | Resource/delivery compensation pool |
| Growth / Talent / R&D | **10%** | Hiring, contractors, training, AI R&D, ESOP/incentives |
| **Gen Clover Corporate** | **25%** | *(sum of the five lines below)* |
| ↳ Corporate Operations | 8% | CA, audit, ROC, legal, accounting, banking, compliance |
| ↳ Technology / Infrastructure | 4% | AI tools, Microsoft 365, GitHub, cloud, security, software |
| ↳ Sales / Marketing | 4% | Lead generation, website, CRM, proposals, marketing, BD |
| ↳ Risk / Working Capital | 4% | Payment delays, FX, bench, bad debt, emergencies |
| ↳ Retained Company Profit | 5% | Actual retained profit / capital accumulation |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

**Universal formula**

```
Client Revenue = Delivery (65%) + Growth/Talent (10%) + Corporate Ops (8%)
               + Technology (4%) + Sales/Marketing (4%) + Risk (4%) + Retained Profit (5%)
```

**For every $100 billed:**

| Delivery | Growth/Talent | Corporate Ops | Technology | Sales/Marketing | Risk | Profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $65 | $10 | $8 | $4 | $4 | $4 | $5 |

> Retained Profit (5%) is **part of** the 25% Corporate bucket. Do not add it on top.

## 3. How the GC Standard Rates Were Set

### 3.1 Method (applied to every role)
1. **Market position.** The Standard Rate sits at about the **40th–50th percentile of the India market range** for 7+ years' experience. That keeps Gen Clover competitive against other Indian vendors without being the cheapest option.
2. **Role ladder.** Related roles follow a fixed pay order, so no role is priced out of line with its peers:
   - **Product & Delivery Mgmt:** Technical Project Manager $55 < Product Manager $60 < Technical Product Manager $65
   - **Analysis & Design:** Business Analyst = UI/UX Designer at $45
   - **Core Engineering:** Frontend $50 < Backend = Mobile at $55 < Full-Stack $60
   - **Quality:** QA (Manual) $35 < Automation QA / SDET $45
   - **Specialist Engineering:** Data $60 < DevOps = Cybersecurity at $65 < AI/LLM $80
   - **Technical Leadership:** Technical Lead $70 (the top engineer rate in the team + $10) < Architect $80
   - **Support:** Documentation / Client Communication $35 (below Business Analyst)
3. **Delivery-cost check.** The 65% Delivery pool must be able to pay a strong 7+ year Indian hire, with room for benefits, leave, bench time and non-billable hours. The target is **Delivery pool ≥ 1.25× the midpoint of the India salary (CTC) range**. The table in 3.2 shows the check.
4. **Rounding.** All rates are rounded to the nearest $5, which makes them easy to quote and remember.

### 3.3 Why the v1 Rates Were Off, and the Reasons for Each Change

**Problems in v1**
- **Product and management roles were underpriced.** At $50/hr, the Product Manager Delivery pool was about ₹55 lakh a year, roughly **1.0×** the salary of a senior Indian PM. That leaves nothing for benefits, bench time or hiring cost, and would have forced Gen Clover to hire below the 7+ year level.
- **Peer roles were out of line.** The Technical Project Manager ($50) was priced the same as the Product Manager and below the Business Analyst-level Documentation role ($45). Automation QA / SDET ($40) was priced as close to manual QA, even though at 7+ years it is an engineering skill.
- **Specialist roles were too low.** DevOps ($60), AI/LLM ($75) and Architect ($75) sat at or below the middle of their markets. AI/ML and DevOps rates have gone up **70–90% since 2024**, and LLM work carries a further **12–40% premium**.
- **Documentation was overpriced.** At $45, a support role cost the same as a Business Analyst or UI/UX Designer.
- **Floors followed no rule.** They ranged from 8% to 17% below the Standard Rate.

**Changes (v1 → v2)**
- **Product Manager $50 → $60:** brings the Delivery-cost check to 1.2× and places the rate at the market midpoint. Gen Clover sells product ownership, so the PM is the role clients see most.
- **Technical Product Manager $55 → $65:** keeps it one step above the PM, as the ladder requires, and reflects the higher salary a technical PM commands (₹45–80 lakh).
- **Technical Project Manager $50 → $55:** puts it above the Business Analyst and one step below the PM. It is a delivery-management role, not a product-strategy role.
- **Automation QA / SDET $40 → $45:** a 7+ year SDET earns ₹25–40 lakh, and at $40 the Delivery-cost check fell to 1.35×. The new rate keeps a clear $10 gap above manual QA.
- **DevOps / Cloud $60 → $65:** now equal to Cybersecurity, since both are scarce infrastructure specialists. It sits at the 43rd percentile of a $50–85 market.
- **Solution Architect $75 → $80:** moves to the market midpoint and keeps a $10 step above the Technical Lead.
- **AI / LLM Engineer $75 → $80:** at $75 the Delivery-cost check was only 1.22×, and this is Gen Clover's key differentiator. LLM specialists should be quoted at the premium rate of $90–95.
- **Documentation / Client Communication $45 → $35:** it is a support role, so it now sits below the Business Analyst.
- **Unchanged:** Business Analyst, UI/UX Designer, Frontend, Backend, Full-Stack, Mobile, QA (Manual), Technical Lead, Data Engineer and Cybersecurity already met all three tests. Full-Stack at $60 remains the anchor for the $60 blended rate.
- **Floors:** now follow one rule: minus $5 up to $65, minus $10 from $70. Every floor still gives a Delivery-cost check of at least 1.0× the salary midpoint.

**How these rates support Gen Clover**
- **Clients still save 34–49%** compared with a loaded US hire, so the value is easy to sell.
- **Gen Clover isn't the cheapest vendor.** It avoids the low-price end of the market, which clients tend to link with body-shop quality. That fits the managed-vendor positioning.
- **The 25% Corporate bucket grows with the more senior roles.** For example, the Architect now contributes $20.00/hr instead of $18.75.
- **Clients can check the rates.** Each rate is tied to a public market range and a US comparison, so a client challenging a quote can be shown the basis.


## 6. Notes & Assumptions

| Topic | Note |
|---|---|
| **Benchmark scope** | 7+ years' experience, India-based senior talent serving US and other international clients. |
| **Pricing philosophy** | Gen Clover is positioned as a managed product/engineering vendor, not a staffing firm. Clients pay for outcomes and ownership, not only for hours. |
| **65/10/25 model** | 65% delivery, 10% growth/talent/R&D, 25% corporate. The 25% is used internally for operations, technology, sales, risk and retained profit. |
| **Client-facing rates** | Never disclose the internal split to the client. Quote the applicable GC Standard Rate, the blended rate or the retainer. |
| **Negotiation floor** | An internal minimum only. Use it only when there's a strategic reason. Never go below the floor, because the Delivery pool would no longer cover a 7+ year hire. |
| **Tight roles** | Product Manager and Technical Product Manager have the least cost headroom. Staff them part-time across clients, as in the sample retainer, rather than as dedicated full-time roles at the floor rate. |
| **Rate review** | Review at least once a year, or whenever Gen Clover's actual costs, team make-up, market rates or the ₹/$ rate change a lot (more than ±10%). |
| **Third-party costs** | Hosting, domains, paid APIs, payment gateway fees and SaaS subscriptions are passed through to the client at cost or cost + handling. They are not included in the hourly rates. |
| **Tax** | Rates are commercial prices before any Indian taxes. Exports of services to US clients are usually zero-rated under GST (with an LUT filed). Confirm with your CA. |
| **US comparison** | US salaries are national averages. In SF, NYC and Seattle they run 20–40% higher. A US staffing agency supplying an onsite contractor usually bills $90–175/hr, so the real savings are often bigger than the table shows. |
| **FX** | Invoice in USD. FX movement is absorbed by the 4% Risk bucket. The cost check assumes ₹88/$. |
| **India salary figures** | The salary ranges in Section 3.2 are indicative, compiled from public 2026 salary guides. Where a guide didn't cover a role at 7+ years, the range is an estimate. Replace them with Gen Clover's actual offer data once you have it. |
